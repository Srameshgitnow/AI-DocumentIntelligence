Company Profile

Company Name: NovaWorks Technologies
Industry: Software and AI Solutions
Location: London, United Kingdom
Founded: 2020

About the company:
NovaWorks Technologies is a UK-based software and AI solutions company that helps businesses automate workflows, process documents, and improve customer support with modern AI tools.

The company focuses on three main services:
1. AI document processing and automation
2. Retrieval-augmented generation for enterprise search
3. Business intelligence dashboards and internal knowledge systems

Key clients:
- Fintech startups
- Legal document processing teams
- Healthcare operations teams
- Logistics and supply chain businesses

Vision:
NovaWorks aims to make advanced AI accessible to businesses that want to improve productivity, reduce manual work, and use data more effectively.
